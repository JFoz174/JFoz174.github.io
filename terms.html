Private use

Jemily Backups is operated by James for personal household backups. It is not a public or commercial backup service. Access is limited to accounts authorised by the operator.

Authorised data

The application may only be used to back up data that the operator owns or has permission to store and process.

Operation and recovery

Backups depend on the availability of the local server, internet connection and Google Drive account. Successful recovery also requires the appropriate encryption password. The operator is responsible for checking backup results, testing restoration and retaining recovery credentials.

Use of Google Drive and GitHub Pages remains subject to those providers' applicable terms and policies.

Discontinuing use

The account owner may stop using the application and revoke its Google account access at any time. Existing backup data remains until separately deleted or removed by retention.
